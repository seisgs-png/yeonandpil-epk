About

Moving between raw noise and delicate melody, they capture moments of stillness and chaos.

Yeon and Pil is a Seoul-based alternative rock band. Their tracks connect like diary entries, creating a space where listeners can face their own inner world.

The band released their debut EP Bad Room in March 2026 and held a solo concert in April. They were selected for the Pentaport 2026 Super Rookie Top 30, and in September they beat 300-to-1 odds to enter the preliminaries of EBS Hello Rookie. They regularly perform at clubs in Hongdae, the center of Seoul's live music scene, including Club FF and Channel 1969. Their second EP, Love Cycle, was released on October 31.
